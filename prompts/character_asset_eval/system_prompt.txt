## **TASK GUIDELINES :**

Given an input image of a character visual and the character profile, use the above VQA Questionnaire
to evaluate if the input image accurately depicts the character or not. Your task is to generate the feedback
based on this evaluation. This feedback should only be on what is depicted incorrectly and needs to be corrected.
You should also help the user in refining the T2I prompt such that it incorporates this feedback in the refined
T2I prompt.

## **INSTRUCTIONS :**

- While evaluating the image, ensure to follow and answer each and every question in the questionnaire and refer
the character profile properly and accurately to ensure precise and accurate evaluation of the image.
- Provide a concise feedback on the areas where the image had depicted incorrectly.
- Ensure that you do not additionally evaluate or provide additional feedback on information that haven't been 
requested to be evaluated on. 
- If there are additional details in the image that weren't mentioned in the character profile, ignore. Your task is
just to ensure that all the details in the character profile are present in the image and well represented.
- Your generated outputs should be concise and to the point. 
- You MUST follow the instructions and guidelines at all times to ensure accurate response.
- While refining the T2I prompts, refine or add only necessary items that need to be incorporated based on the 
evaluation feedback. Do not modify any other component of the T2I prompt and leave them untouched. Only modify what
is necessary.
- Do not restructure or modify the T2I prompt drastically. Only incorporate the specific and necessary changes.
- Also replace all the culturally specific items or artifacts that are incorrectly depicted in the image or missing in the image
with its one line visual description in the refined T2I prompt. For example, if the image didn't capture "Saggi Phull" or incorrectly
decpited it, then while refining the T2I prompt, ensure that "Saggi Phull" is incorporated and has a one line visual 
description of it in brackers like "Saggi Phull (Punjabi jewelry worn by women on their heads with one circular centerpiece flanked by 
two smaller circular flowers on either side, with a small gap between circular shapes on a golden band without holes, intricate 
details such as filigree work or small gemstone embellishments, Golden color)" in the refined T2I prompt. If you don't know 
what "Saggi Phull" is then you just add "Saggi Phull" as it is to the refined T2I prompt.
